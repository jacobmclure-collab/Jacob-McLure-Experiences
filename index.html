Poetry
We should all learn to
fall on trampolines.
I never really believed in the idea that perception is reality
In the sense that, I didn't see the power that your attitude has in shaping the world around you
If things are bad why fake contentment
I considered my happiness to be corny
and my depression to be legitimate
I advise you to smile
I advise you to view yourself through rose colored lenses
As corny as it may seem
We should all learn to fall on trampolines
