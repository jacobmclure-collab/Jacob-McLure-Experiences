About

The content that actually moves people isn't the content that follows the playbook.

I'm a creative professional who's spent the last four years at the intersection of sports media, digital marketing, and storytelling. I built a community of 104,000 people from nothing. I managed an NFL athlete's brand for nearly four years. I had my poetry displayed at UC Berkeley. I worked on go-to-market strategy for Fear of God Athletics at adidas.

Right now I'm looking for opportunities across media, marketing, and analytics — teams that operate at the highest level and want someone who can build the story and measure it.
